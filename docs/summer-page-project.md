# Summer School Evergreen Page Project

## Project purpose

Redesign the permanent `/summer` page so it performs two jobs well:

1. Reflect on and share the Summer School experience through meaningful photos, short videos, and written stories.
2. Remain an evergreen, indexable marketing page that builds trust and generates interest in the next Summer School.

The page should help a prospective parent understand:

- What Summer School at Luana feels like.
- What children do and learn.
- Whether the program is suitable for their child.
- How to receive information about the next program.

## Current state

- The permanent page is `summer.html`, published at `/summer`.
- The current page is primarily an archive of the 2026 schedule, themes, fees, daily timetable, and FAQs.
- It correctly states that Summer School 2026 has ended.
- The repository contains many photos from July 2026, including optimized WebP versions.
- No local Summer School video files were found during the initial review.
- The permanent `/summer` URL should be retained to preserve search history and avoid splitting authority across yearly URLs.
- The existing page remains unchanged while this project is in planning.

## Proposed audience

Primary audience:

- Parents of kindergarten and elementary-school-aged children in Fuchu, Nakagawara, Tama, and the Seiseki-Sakuragaoka area.
- Parents looking for an English-rich summer experience.
- Parents whose children are new to English as well as those already studying English.

Secondary audience:

- Families who participated and want to revisit or share the experience.
- Families considering Luana's regular classes.

## Content principles

- Lead with the experience and children's growth, not expired logistics.
- Use real activities, observations, photographs, and parent comments as evidence.
- Give photos and videos meaningful captions so the page remains useful and indexable.
- Clearly distinguish historical 2026 information from future program information.
- Do not publish unconfirmed dates, fees, themes, or availability for the next year.
- Keep the page useful throughout the entire year.
- Obtain and respect permission for every identifiable child shown in public media.

## Proposed page structure

### 1. Experience-led hero

Use one strong photograph or a short muted video loop.

Working H1:

> 府中市の英語サマースクール｜Luana English School

Working introduction:

> 英語で遊び、作り、探検する。年中・年長・小学生が、テーマ活動やフィールドトリップを通して成長する特別な夏です。

Primary actions:

- `2026年の思い出を見る`
- `次回開催のお知らせを受け取る`

Evergreen status message:

> 2026年度は終了しました｜2027年度の案内は決まり次第お知らせします

### 2. Latest Summer highlights

A short proof bar summarizing confirmed program highlights, such as:

- Four themed weeks
- English throughout the day
- Creative projects and games
- Special field trips
- Beginners welcome

Only confirmed figures and claims should be used.

### 3. Our Summer Story

This will be the main photo-led reflection. Rather than a large undifferentiated gallery, selected media should be grouped into three to five short stories:

- Making and creating
- Learning through play
- Working together
- Exploring beyond the classroom
- Celebrating and sharing what we learned

Each story should contain:

- Two to four selected photographs.
- A short Japanese reflection.
- A sentence explaining the English-learning or developmental value.
- An optional child quote or teacher observation.

Working reflection style:

> 制作活動では、色や形、道具の名前を英語で確認しながら、自分のアイデアを作品にしました。完成した作品を英語で紹介する姿にも成長が見られました。

### 4. Short video moments

Use approximately three to five short clips rather than one long recap. Possible subjects:

- Morning circle
- Theme activity
- Group game
- Project presentation
- Field-trip moment

Each video should include:

- A poster image.
- A visible title and Japanese caption.
- Playback controls.
- Captions or an adjacent transcript when speech is important.
- No autoplay with sound.

The hosting approach still needs to be selected. Options include locally hosted optimized MP4 files or an appropriate third-party video platform.

### 5. What children gain

Translate the activities into parent-facing outcomes, supported by examples elsewhere on the page:

- Confidence using English.
- The ability to express ideas.
- Creativity and problem solving.
- Teamwork and new friendships.
- Independence in a new environment.

### 6. A typical day

Retain the useful daily timeline in evergreen form:

`Arrival → Circle Time → Theme Activity → Lunch → Project or Trip → English Diary → Goodbye`

Pair the timeline with two or three relevant photos. Year-specific dates should not appear in this section.

### 7. Past program themes

Preserve the 2026 program as a clearly labelled archive. For each week, show:

- Theme.
- One-sentence activity summary.
- One highlight or field trip.
- Optional representative photo.

Historical dates may appear as secondary information. Expired prices should be removed from the primary page flow or placed in a clearly labelled archive disclosure if there is a strong reason to retain them.

### 8. Parent and child voices

Add approximately three concise, permission-cleared testimonials. Useful themes include:

- What a child talked about at home.
- A change or growth a parent noticed.
- How a child new to English responded.

Anonymous attribution such as `小学2年生 保護者` is acceptable.

### 9. Next Summer call to action

Working heading:

> 次の夏を、英語で特別な思い出に。

Possible actions:

- Join the priority notification list for the next Summer School.
- Contact Luana through LINE.
- Book a trial for a current regular class.

A notification form should remain short:

- Parent or guardian name.
- Email address or preferred contact method.
- Child's current school year.
- Optional question.

The exact form and data-handling method must be confirmed before implementation.

### 10. Evergreen FAQ

Candidate questions:

- 英語初心者でも参加できますか？
- 外部生も参加できますか？
- 対象年齢は？
- 何日から参加できますか？
- 昼食や持ち物は？
- 延長利用はできますか？
- サマースクールの募集はいつ始まりますか？
- 府中市外・多摩市からも参加できますか？

Answers must avoid promising details that have not been confirmed for the next program.

### Technical content requirements

- Keep the canonical URL as `https://luanaenglishschool.jp/summer`.
- Give every meaningful photograph descriptive Japanese alt text.
- Give every video a title and text description.
- Use appropriate responsive image sizes and optimized media.
- Update structured data only where it accurately represents visible content.
- Update the sitemap modification date at production release.
- Create an accurate social-sharing image after the final design and media selection are stable.

## Project workflow

### Phase 1: Content and media inventory

- Review the existing Summer School page in detail.
- Shortlist approximately 12–18 strong photos.
- Select approximately three to five short videos.
- Confirm the 2026 weekly themes and activity details.
- Gather teacher reflections and two or three parent comments.
- Confirm permissions for all public media and testimonials.
- Decide how families should register interest in the next program.

Deliverable: approved content and media shortlist.

### Phase 2: Copy draft

- Draft all Japanese headings and body copy.
- Draft story captions and learning reflections.
- Draft benefits, testimonials, FAQ answers, and CTA copy.
- Finalize the SEO title and description.
- Mark every fact that requires confirmation.

Deliverable: complete reviewable page copy.

### Phase 3: Visual prototype

- Create a dedicated Git branch for the project.
- Build the redesigned responsive page using representative approved media.
- Add accessible video components and the interest CTA.
- Preserve the site's established branding, navigation, and permanent URL.
- Produce a private or local review version without changing production.

Deliverable: working prototype.

### Phase 4: Structured review and refinement

Review:

- Photo selection and ordering.
- Accuracy and tone of Japanese copy.
- Representation of the actual program.
- Child media permissions.
- Mobile usability.
- Interest or application path.
- Historical versus future information.

Deliverable: approved production candidate.

### Phase 5: Production validation

- Verify desktop and mobile layout.
- Optimize image and video loading.
- Verify accessibility, alt text, captions, links, and forms.
- Verify metadata, canonical URL, social preview, and structured data.
- Run the site's automated tests.
- Confirm there are no regressions elsewhere on the site.

Deliverable: tested release candidate.

### Phase 6: Production release

- Commit the approved page and assets.
- Merge the project branch into `main`.
- Push the release through the existing production workflow.
- Verify the live `/summer` page and its main conversion path.
- Request re-indexing through Google Search Console if available.

Deliverable: verified live page.

## Annual operating model

Before registration opens:

- Show memories from the latest program.
- Invite families to join the notification list.

When registration opens:

- Add confirmed dates, themes, fees, availability, and application action.

During Summer School:

- Optionally add a small number of permission-cleared highlights.

After Summer School:

- Replace registration details with the new retrospective.
- Return the main CTA to next-year notifications.
- Preserve prior years in a concise archive when useful.
